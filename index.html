    How it works for venues

    Questions

    Who can get a card?

    IE University students in Segovia only. You need your IE student email, ending @student.ie.edu, to get one. A venue may ask to see your student ID next to the card, and the names have to match.

    How does a venue check it?

    Your card carries a QR code. The venue points any phone camera at it and a page opens showing whether the card is valid, the name on it and the date it runs out. They need no app and no account.

    What if I have no signal at the bar?

    Once you have opened your card on your phone, it is saved there and opens without a connection. The venue's phone needs a connection the first time it checks a card.

    What happens at the end of term?

    The card expires on the last day of term. You buy a new one for the next term.

    Is this live?

    Not yet. This is a working prototype built for a student pitch. You can issue yourself a card and scan it, but no money is taken, no venue has agreed a discount yet, and the prototype cannot yet confirm that an IE email address is really yours.
